# 🎵 SAREGAMA – Digital Ecosystem for Music Entertainment Optimization

### Digital Transformation, Platform Strategy & Ecosystem Design

This project proposes a digital ecosystem for SAREGAMA designed to transform its traditional catalogue-based business model into a dynamic, multi-sided digital platform.

The proposed ecosystem connects artists, listeners, distributors, advertisers, content creators, curators, and B2B partners through an API-driven architecture designed to improve music discovery, consumption, monetization, and cultural heritage preservation.

> **Academic Team Project | Digital Ecosystems | GITAM School of Business | November 2025**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The project explores how SAREGAMA can evolve from a traditional music content owner into an **Intelligent Music Ecosystem Orchestrator**.

The proposed ecosystem focuses on:

- AI-powered music discovery and personalization
- API-driven platform architecture
- Multi-sided stakeholder ecosystem
- Digital rights and royalty management
- Diverse monetization models
- Data-driven insights
- Creator economy enablement
- Platform scalability
- Sustainable digital transformation
- Cultural heritage preservation

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The proposed ecosystem aims to:

- Optimize music discovery through AI-powered recommendations
- Improve the digital consumption experience
- Create new and diversified revenue streams
- Enable artists and creators to manage and monetize their content
- Improve rights and royalty management
- Connect multiple stakeholder groups through a unified platform
- Preserve SAREGAMA's musical heritage through digitization and cultural documentation
- Build a scalable and sustainable digital platform

## 🏗️ Platform Architecture

The proposed platform follows a **five-layer modular, API-first architecture**.

### 1. Data Foundation Layer

- Music catalogue database
- User behavioural data
- Rights management ledger
- Cultural context engine

### 2. Core Platform Services

- Identity and access management
- Content delivery network
- AI-powered recommendation engine
- Analytics and insights
- Payment and settlement system

### 3. API Gateway Layer

- Public APIs
- Partner APIs
- Creator APIs
- Consumer APIs

### 4. Application Layer

- SAREGAMA streaming application
- Artist portal
- Business intelligence suite
- Licensing marketplace

### 5. Integration Layer

- External streaming platforms
- Social media platforms
- Smart devices and IoT
- Payment gateways

## 🔄 Key Data Flows

The proposed ecosystem includes four major data flows:

### Content Ingestion

Artist → Upload API → Quality Check → Metadata Enrichment → Rights Assignment → Catalogue Publication → Distribution

### Music Discovery

User Profile → Behavioural Analysis → Recommendation Algorithm → Catalogue Query → Personalized Playlist → Streaming

### Monetization

Stream Event → Usage Tracking → Revenue Attribution → Royalty Calculation → Smart Contract Execution → Payment Distribution

### Licensing

License Request → Catalogue Search → Rights Verification → Quote Generation → Contract Management → Usage Monitoring

## 👥 Stakeholder Ecosystem

The platform is designed as a multi-sided ecosystem connecting six major stakeholder groups:

| Stakeholder | Key Value |
|---|---|
| Artists & Creators | Content distribution, analytics, monetization and royalty management |
| Listeners | Personalized discovery and enhanced music consumption |
| Distributors | Catalogue licensing and API-based integration |
| Advertisers & Brands | Targeted advertising and audience insights |
| Content Curators | Playlist creation, visibility and revenue opportunities |
| B2B Partners | Music licensing, integrations and co-branded opportunities |

## 💰 Monetization Strategy

The proposed ecosystem uses a diversified monetization model with seven revenue streams:

1. Consumer subscriptions
2. Advertising revenue
3. Licensing and synchronization rights
4. Artist services and tools
5. Data and insights
6. Live events and experiences
7. B2B platform licensing

The project also explores dynamic pricing and personalization based on geography, behavioural patterns, seasonal campaigns, and loyalty programs.

## 📊 Financial & Unit Economics
